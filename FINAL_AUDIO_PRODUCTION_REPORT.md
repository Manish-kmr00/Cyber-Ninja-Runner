## 3. Music Stem Synchronization Analysis

> [!NOTE]
> **Status Tag:** `MUSIC_STEMS_REQUIRES_REVIEW`
> 
> **Analysis Findings:**
> The three gameplay stems (`music_gameplay_base.wav`, `music_gameplay_intensity.wav`, `music_gameplay_danger.wav`) have identical lengths (2,112,000 frames @ 48 kHz = 44.0 seconds) and consistent cyberpunk synthwave instrumentation. However, because they were generated independently by the AI generation engine, their downbeats, transient accents, and harmonic chord modulations were synthesized separately rather than rendered from a single locked DAW master clock.
> 
> In accordance with the audio pipeline guidelines:
> - Synchronization is **NOT faked**.
> - The files are ingested on separate group tracks within `event:/Music/Gameplay` in FMOD Studio, ready for live crossfading or manual tempo-alignment in post-production if true sample-lock is required.
